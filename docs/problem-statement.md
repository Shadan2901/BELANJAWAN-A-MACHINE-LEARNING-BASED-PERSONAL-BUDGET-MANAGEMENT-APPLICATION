# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
University students are the primary users affected by this problem. They often depend on limited financial resources such as family allowances, scholarships, or part-time employment to cover their daily expenses. University support services may also be affected because students' financial difficulties can influence their well-being.

## What exactly is going wrong?
University students may struggle to monitor their daily expenses and manage their monthly budgets because their financial transactions are not always recorded in one consistent place. Without a clear summary of their income and spending, they may find it difficult to identify repeated expenses, understand which categories consume most of their allowance, and keep track of their savings.

## Why does it matter?
Poor expense tracking and budget management can lead to overspending, difficulties managing limited allowances, and failure to achieve savings goals. Students may also develop poor financial management habits when they cannot clearly understand their spending patterns. Therefore, they need an accessible way to record transactions, review expenses, and plan their savings.
