# BELANJAWAN: A Machine Learning Based Personal Budget Management Application

## Project Summary
Belanjawan is a proposed personal budget management application designed to help university students record their income and expenses, manage monthly budgets, track savings goals and analyse spending patterns using Machine Learning.

## Problem Statement
University students may struggle to monitor their daily expenses and manage a limited monthly budget because their financial transactions are not always recorded in one consistent place. Without a clear summary of income and spending, students may not notice repeated expenses or identify categories that use a large part of their allowance. This can lead to poor financial management and difficulties in achieving savings goals.

Therefore, students need an accessible way to record transactions, review spending patterns and plan savings to make better financial decisions.

## Specialized Technology
**Machine Learning (ML)**

The proposed application uses Machine Learning techniques to analyse historical financial records, identify spending patterns and estimate future monthly expenses.

Methods and tools:
- Linear Regression
- K-Means Clustering
- Decision Tree
- Python scikit-learn
